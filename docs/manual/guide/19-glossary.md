# Glossary

The words this documentation uses, in alphabetical order.

Banner
:   The bar along the top of the screen. It shows the open window, the clock and the pills. Also called the top bar. See [The top bar](#guide-top-bar).

Box
:   The large panel that opens from the dock with a tap of <kbd>Super</kbd>: your apps, a search, files, settings and a terminal, all in one place. See [Apps and the dock](#guide-dock).

Card
:   A small shelf that rides beside a window: you keep text, files and pictures on it while you work, and paste them into the window with a click. See [The card](#guide-card).

Controls
:   The settings of StandardOS, one tile per setting. Open it by tapping <kbd>Super</kbd> and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> together, from the dock, or from the gear's **More…**. See [Controls](#guide-controls).

Dock
:   The row of app icons at the bottom of the screen. It steps aside when a window needs its place, and comes back when you touch the bottom edge with the pointer. See [Apps and the dock](#guide-dock).

Generation
:   One complete version of the system as it was built on your computer. Every update and every rebuild makes a new one; the boot menu lists the recent ones, so you can start an older one.

Golem
:   The one who works behind StandardOS: it keeps the computer up to date, repairs it, and tells you what it did. Golem uses the same tools you do, and has no power you do not have. See [Golem](#guide-golem).

Machine folder
:   `/etc/nixos`, the folder that describes your computer. For experts: see [The machine folder](#expert-machine-folder).

Module
:   A whole kind of work in one switch — gaming, development, music, design… — with its programs and the system parts they need. See [Installing apps](#guide-apps).

OPTIONS
:   How StandardOS offers you things: the right control at the right moment, and nothing in the way when you do not need it. The pills of the top bar are OPTIONS.

Palette
:   A set of colours the whole desktop wears: the dock, the boxes, the apps, the terminal, the browser's chrome. StandardOS ships its own palette, called Standard, and many more. See [Controls](#guide-controls).

Pill
:   A small rounded control in the top bar: the clock, the bell, the gear, the card's pill, and others that appear when they have something to show.

Seam
:   The web browser of StandardOS: Firefox, set up to fit the desktop. Web apps open in Seam too.

Stable
:   The version of StandardOS that installed computers follow: published once a week, after it passed its tests.

Standard, StandardOS
:   The system: everything that is installed, has a version, and starts when you press the power button. "StandardOS" is its full name; "Standard" is what we call it day to day.

Tiles, tiling
:   A way of placing windows side by side so they share the screen without overlapping. StandardOS can tile windows or let them float. See [Windows](#guide-windows).

Web app
:   A website that opens in a window of its own, with its own icon in the dock, like an app.
