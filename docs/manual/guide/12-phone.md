# Your phone

Plug an Android phone in with its cable and StandardOS recognises it: a phone pill appears on the top bar and its icon on the desktop. From there you can open its files, import its photos, see its screen on the computer, use its camera as a webcam, share its internet, and keep its photos and files in sync over Wi-Fi.

## The first time

A phone shows its files only when it allows it. If its icon stands closed, its **Open** says what to do on the phone:

1. Unlock the phone's screen.
2. When the phone asks, choose **File transfer** (some phones call it MTP).
3. For the screen mirror and the camera, also turn on **USB debugging**: on the phone, open Settings → About phone, tap *Build number* seven times, then open Developer options and switch on *USB debugging*. The phone pill's box walks you through it. When the phone asks *"Allow USB debugging?"*, tap **Allow**.

## What you can do

Right-click the phone's icon on the desktop (or in Files' sidebar), or scroll down on its pill:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Its files, in Standard Files |
| **Import photos** | Copies the photos you do not have yet into **Phones → <name> → Photos** |
| **Mirror screen** | The phone's screen in a window, controlled with your mouse and keyboard |
| **Use as camera** | The phone becomes a webcam for video calls; choose the lens: ultra wide, main, telephoto or front |
| **Use phone's internet** | The computer connects through the phone's mobile data (over the cable) |
| **Configure…** | A name for the phone, its folder, and Wi-Fi sync (below) |
| **Properties** | Model, Android version, battery and storage |
| **Eject** | Before unplugging |

Dropping files on the phone's icon copies them into its **Download** folder. Clicking the phone pill also ejects the phone.

## Sync over Wi-Fi

In **Configure…**, give the phone a name: everything from it goes to one folder, **Phones → <name>**, with Photos, Music, Downloads, Documents, Recordings and WhatsApp inside. Then switch on **Sync over Wi-Fi** and **Apply** while the phone is plugged in. From then on, whenever the phone is on the same Wi-Fi, StandardOS copies what is new — at the interval you chose, when the phone arrives, or only while it charges.

**Clean the phone** moves what you choose off the phone: each file is deleted from the phone only after its copy on the computer has been checked.

## Bluetooth

To send files to a phone, or receive them, without a cable: open **Controls → Bluetooth**, pair the phone, then use **Send or receive files**. Received files go to **Downloads**.
