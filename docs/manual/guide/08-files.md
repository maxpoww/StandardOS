# Files and the desktop

Your files live in your home folder: Documents, Downloads, Pictures, Music, Videos, Desktop — named in your language. **Standard Files** is the app to browse them; the desktop shows the files of your Desktop folder; and **Quick Look** shows any file big with a press of <kbd>Space</kbd>.

## Standard Files

Open it from the box: type *files* and choose **Standard Files**. (GNOME's Files, a second file manager, may show up beside it.)

- **The sidebar** on the left opens when the pointer reaches it (the pin keeps it open): **Files** or **Media** (only pictures and videos), your favourite places — Recents, your home, your folders, your phones — and the disks, plugged-in sticks and the Bin.
- **The top line** has tabs, the path (click a step to go there; ✎ or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>L</kbd> to type a path), **↶ undo** (when something can be undone), sorting, the **i** card with a file's details, grid or list, hidden files, and search.
- **To search,** just start typing.
- **Select** files and a pill appears at the bottom: Copy · Move · Rename · Open with · New folder with these · Copy to the stick · Bin · ⋯ (more). After **Copy**, go to another folder and click **Copy here**.
- **Smart buttons** depend on what you select: songs and videos get *Play*; a photo gets *Set as wallpaper*, *Rotate*, *Slideshow*; a PDF gets *Print*; an archive gets *Extract here*.
- **Drag and drop** files between folders, onto the desktop, onto the dock's Bin, or into another app. Hold a file over a folder to spring it open.

### What Files protects

Your own files are yours: move, rename and delete them freely — the Bin and undo catch mistakes. A few places are *delicate* (your settings folders, your main folders themselves, your phones' folders): Files asks once before changing them. The system's own folders can be read but never changed from Files: *"This is the system's. Golem manages it."*

### The Bin

Deleted files go to the Bin (it is shared with every app). In the Bin, **Put back** returns a file to where it was, and **Delete for good** removes it (click twice). Files older than 30 days are emptied from the Bin by themselves.

## Quick Look

Select a file and press <kbd>Space</kbd>: it opens big, growing out of its icon — in Files, on the desktop, or in the box's search results. Pictures (including camera RAW, HEIC and moving GIFs), PDFs page by page, videos and songs (they play), fonts, archives (their list), office documents, web pages, text and code, Markdown, tables. The arrow keys go to the next file; <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes; <kbd>Enter</kbd> opens it in its app.

**The pencil** lets you change it right there:

- **Pictures:** crop, draw, add text, blur, tint (brightness, contrast, warmth, black and white), turn. **Save** keeps the original in a safe place; **Save copy** writes "*name (edited)*" beside it.
- **PDFs:** highlight, draw, add text, and sign (your signature is drawn once and kept).
- **Text:** edit it in place (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd> saves).
- **Videos and songs:** cut the start and the end; take a video's sound away; fade a song in and out.

## The desktop

The desktop shows the files of your **Desktop** folder, each in the place you put it.

- Click to select, double-click to open, drag to move. Drag a rubber band on the background to select several.
- **A click on the bare background hides all the icons** (or shows them again).
- Plugged-in sticks and phones appear on the right side of the desktop.
- Right-click the background for: undo, paste, new folder, sort, clean up, hide icons, next wallpaper, Wallpapers…, Colours…, Display….

## The one menu

Every right-click in StandardOS — on a file, a folder, the desktop, a stick, a phone — opens the same kind of menu, in the same order: **Open · Copy and Move · Change · Send · Keep · Get info**, and the way out last (**Move to Bin**, or **Eject**). A row with **›** turns the menu's page (*Open with ›*, *Send to ›*, *Sort by ›*); a row with **…** opens a window; a **red** row cannot be undone. The arrow keys and <kbd>Enter</kbd> work in every menu, and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>+</kbd> / <kbd>−</kbd> make every menu bigger or smaller.

*Open with › Another app* lists every app that can open the file, with a search and **Always use for this kind of file**.

## USB sticks and memory cards

They are ready by themselves when plugged in: in Files' sidebar, on the desktop and as a drive pill on the bar. **Copy to <stick>** appears in the selection pill. To remove one safely, click its drive pill's round part, choose **Eject** in its menu, or drag it to the Bin (that ejects it, it never deletes anything). Formatting, renaming and checking a drive are in **Controls → Storage**.
