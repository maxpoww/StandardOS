# The top bar

The bar along the top of the screen shows what you need to know now, and nothing else. Its round buttons — *pills* — appear only when they have something to say: the phone pill only while a phone is plugged in, the task pill only while something long is running.

Two habits work on every pill: **hover** it to see more, and **scroll down** on it to open its box (scroll up, or move away, to fold it). <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>+</kbd> and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>−</kbd> with the pointer on the bar make the whole bar bigger or smaller; <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>0</kbd> puts it back.

## From left to right

### The gear — quick settings

At the far left. A click opens its box: volume, brightness, microphone, and switches for night light, dark mode, do not disturb, airplane mode, keep awake, floating windows and power mode. At the bottom: a sleep timer, Lock, and Power (hibernate, sleep, restart). **More…** opens [Controls](#guide-controls).

Rest the pointer on the gear and small symbols slide out beside it: the computer's readings — Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, disk, processor, memory, graphics, battery. Their colour says how each one is doing — blue for connected, green for fine, yellow for worth a look, red for off or at its limit. Rest the pointer on one to read it ("Home · 78%"); click it to open its page.

### The window pill

In the middle: the name of the window you are working in.

- **×** (left of the name) closes the window; **minimize** slides out beside it when the pointer is on the ×.
- Hover the name to see the window's mode buttons: tile in place (pseudo), float, fullscreen. When a window is not plainly tiled, a symbol shows its state; a click on it puts the window back in the layout. Floating windows also get a **pin**: on top of everything and on every desk.
- **Click the name** to go to the next window of this desk (most used first); right-click goes the other way.
- At sunset it may ask *"The sun is set, do you want to turn on eye protection?"* — **turn on** warms the screen for the night. Right-click it for "Not now".

### The card's pill

A small round pill that turns [the card](#guide-card) on or off for the window you are in. It also changes for a moment when something happens:

- after a screenshot, it shows the picture small, with **×** to delete it; click it to cut out a part or copy its text (see [Screenshots](#guide-screenshots));
- while you dictate with <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Space</kbd>, it becomes a microphone;
- in the browser, a **copy link** pill slides out of it: one click copies the page's address.

### Pills that come and go

| Pill | When | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Power** | After a short press of the power key | Hibernate, and Sleep and Restart behind it |
| **Task** | While something long runs: an install, a copy, a print, a recording | Shows what and how far along; **Cancel** stops it; scroll down to see every task |
| **Media** | While something plays | A small live spectrum; hover for ⏮ ⏯ ⏭ and what is playing |
| **Drive** | A USB stick or memory card is plugged in | Hover for its free space; click the round part to eject it, the long part to open it |
| **Phone** | A phone is plugged in | Hover for its state ("Synced 2 h ago"); **a click ejects it**; scroll down for its menu (see [Your phone](#guide-phone)) |
| **Terminal** | The box's terminal has tabs | Hover to see the tabs; click one to open it |
| **Sleep timer** | A sleep timer is running | The time left; click it to change it, × to cancel |
| **Capture** | The capture tool is open | Region · Window · Screen · Video · Timer · Copy text |

### The bell — notifications

Amber when there is something new (red when the battery is low). Hover it to read the newest; a new notification also shows by itself for a moment. Scroll on it for the history (it survives restarts); click a notification to open what it is about. The small pill behind the bell turns **Do not disturb** on and off: notifications then wait quietly in the bell.

### The clock

At the far right. Hover it for the date. The clock shows 24 hours unless you choose 12 hours in **Controls → Time zone**.
