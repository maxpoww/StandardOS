# Printers and scanners

Plug a printer in, or turn one on in your network, and it works: StandardOS finds it, sets it up with the right driver, and tells you *"Printer ready — you can print from any app."* The first printer becomes the default.

## Printing

Print from any app's print dialog, or select files in Standard Files (or on the desktop) and use **Print**. Every print shows on the task pill while it is on its way, with **Cancel**. If the printer has a problem — out of paper, turned off, paused — the task pill says so while you are printing. A print that fails ends in a notification.

## Controls → Printers

- The printer's state and, for most printers, its ink.
- **Colour** or black and white, **Quality** (draft, normal, best), **Paper**, **Both sides**.
- The prints waiting (**Cancel**), and the ones from the last hour (**Print again**).
- **Pause / Resume**, **Make default**, **Test page**, **Remove**.
- **Add a printer by its address**, for a network printer that was not found by itself.

Printers that StandardOS supports out of the box: every printer that prints without a driver (most made after 2015: IPP Everywhere, AirPrint), HP, Canon, Epson and Brother (through Gutenprint and their own drivers), Brother lasers, Samsung and Xerox.

## Scanning

When a scanner is found (a multifunction printer's, or one on its own), **Controls → Printers** gets a **Scanners** tab. **Scan** opens Simple Scan, which StandardOS installs by itself the first time a scanner appears.
