# The card, the clipboard and emoji

The card is a small shelf that rides beside a window. Keep on it what you need for that piece of work — text, files, pictures, voice notes — and click any item to paste it into the window. It also holds your clipboard's history, a dictionary, a translator, emoji and voice-to-text.

## Opening the card

- Click the **card** button at the right end of a window's title bar, or the card's pill on the top bar: the card opens for the window you are in.
- <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>.</kbd> opens it on the clipboard, <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>,</kbd> on emoji, <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>/</kbd> on your sessions.

The card sits inside its window, on the right, and hides while you work in another window. Slide it sideways by scrolling over the title bar; a quick flick puts it away; its edges resize it. With the pointer on it, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>+</kbd> / <kbd>−</kbd> / <kbd>0</kbd> change its size.

## What you keep on it

- **Drag anything in**: text, files, pictures, from any app. Files are kept as links to where they are, never copied.
- **Click an item** to paste it into the window. **Drag it out** to drop a copy somewhere.
- **Write** in the card's writing box: <kbd>Enter</kbd> keeps what you wrote on the card, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> sends it straight into the window, <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> starts a new line.
- **Hold <kbd>Space</kbd>** on the card to record a voice note for as long as you hold it.

## Sessions and pins

What you keep for a window is a **session**, named after the window's title. When you open a window with the same title another day, its session comes back. **Sessions** lists them all, like chats; the pencil renames one, the × forgets it (click twice; *Undo* brings it back). **Pinned** keeps copies of items for good, whatever session they came from.

## The pages

| Page | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Clipboard** | Everything you copied, newest first. Click to paste, × to delete, + to keep it in a session. |
| **Dictionary** | Type a word; every language that has it answers (English and Spanish, with etymology). Offline. |
| **Translator** | Type text; it is kept with its translation. English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian. Offline. |
| **Emoji** | Every emoji, with a search (in Spanish too when the desktop is in Spanish), your recent ones first. A click types it into the window. |
| **Voice to text** | Talk; your words are written into the window. |
| **Captures** | Buttons for a screenshot and a screen recording. |

## Dictation

Hold <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Space</kbd> and talk; let go, and your words are typed into the window. A short tap of <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Space</kbd> opens the card on Voice to text instead. <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels while the words are being written. Everything is done on your computer: your voice never leaves it.

## Voice notes

A voice note shows its length and a play button; click its line to play from there, change the speed (×1, ×1.5, ×0.75), give it a title, or **Transcribe** it into text. A recording of only silence, or with the microphone muted, is not kept — the card tells you.

## Copied links

When you copy a link, the clipboard shows it as a card with the page's title and picture. To get them, StandardOS asks that page for its title and picture once — the same request your browser makes when you open the page.

!!! tip "Pasting in a terminal"
    Terminals paste with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd> instead of <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd>.
