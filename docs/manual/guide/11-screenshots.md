# Screenshots and recording

Press <kbd>Print</kbd>: the screen is taken at once, copied to the clipboard and saved in **Pictures → Screenshots**. A small preview appears on the top bar — click it to cut out a part, or leave it and it is kept whole. Hold <kbd>Print</kbd> to record the screen instead.

## The Print key

| Keys | What happens |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Print</kbd> | The screen under the pointer, at once: clipboard + Pictures → Screenshots + a preview on the bar |
| Hold <kbd>Print</kbd> 1.5 seconds | Records the screen instead (from 0.3 s the bar says *"Keep holding to record the screen"*) |
| <kbd>Print</kbd> while recording | Stops the recording |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Print</kbd> | The whole screen, with a notification |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Print</kbd> | The window you are in, with a notification |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Print</kbd> | Start or stop recording the screen |

## The preview

After a screenshot, the card's pill on the top bar shows the picture small, for four seconds (the time stands still while the pointer is on it). Hover it to see it bigger.

- **Click it** to open the editor: choose a **Region**, a **Window** or the whole **Screen**, **Copy text** (it reads the words in the picture), then **Save**.
- **×** deletes the screenshot.
- Leave it alone and it is kept as it is.

## The capture tool

For more choices before the shot — a region you draw, a window, a timer, a video with sound — open the capture tool from the card's **Captures** page, or set **Controls → Screenshots → Print takes → Let me choose first**. Its pills on the top bar offer Region · Window · Screen · Video · Timer (3 or 10 seconds) · Copy text · Sound; the button beside your selection takes it. <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the tool.

## Recordings

A recording shows on the task pill with **Stop**, and is saved in **Videos → Screencasts**. A notification says where; a click opens the folder. Choose silent or with sound in **Controls → Screenshots**.

## Choosing what a screenshot holds

In **Controls → Screenshots**:

- **Print takes:** the whole screen at once, or *Let me choose first*.
- **In the picture:** the top bar, the dock (leaving it out takes a moment longer), the pointer.
- **After a shot:** copy to the clipboard, show the preview.
- **Screen recording:** silent or with sound.
