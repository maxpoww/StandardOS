# Controls

Controls holds every setting of StandardOS, one tile per setting, always in the same place. Every choice applies at once — no restart, no "Apply" — and is kept for the next time.

## Opening Controls

- Tap <kbd>Super</kbd> and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> together, then let go.
- Or click the **Control panel** tile in the dock.
- Or click the gear at the top left, then **More…**.
- Or press <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>H</kbd>, type the setting's name and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>: that one setting opens in a box in the middle of the screen.

Typing in Controls filters the tiles by name and by what people call them (*wifi*, *dark*, *password*, *printer*…); <kbd>Enter</kbd> opens the best match. Arrows and <kbd>Tab</kbd> move between choices; <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> picks.

## The quick settings (the gear)

Most daily switches do not need Controls at all. The gear's box has:

- **Sound and screen:** volume (click its symbol to mute) with the output to play through, the equalizer, brightness (with *Auto* where the computer has a light sensor), and the microphone.
- **Modes:** night light, dark mode, do not disturb, airplane mode, keep awake, floating windows, power mode.
- **At the bottom:** a sleep timer (it hibernates after the minutes you say, and warns you a minute before), Lock, and Power.

## Every setting

### How it looks

| Setting | What you choose |
|---|---|
| **Colours** | The palette the whole desktop wears (*Dynamic* takes colours from your wallpaper; more than a hundred palettes; or make your own with **+**). Separately for the shell, the apps, the icons and the web: the lighting from dark to light, following the sun or not, saturation and transparency. Some apps need to be reopened to change colour. |
| **Wallpaper** | A picture from your **Pictures → Wallpapers** folder (videos work too), or **Follows your colours**: a wallpaper made from your palette. |
| **Rounded corners** | Round or square corners, for everything or part by part: windows, menus, the dock, icons, the bar, Files. |
| **Tiles** | The space between windows; which windows get a title bar; buttons left or right; coloured or grey buttons. |
| **Launcher** | Where the dock goes (left, bottom, right), whether it stays away until called, animated icons, how apps are arranged, recent files. |

### The screen and the sound

| Setting | What you choose |
|---|---|
| **Resolution** | The screen's resolution. A new one is only *tried*: if you do nothing, it goes back after 15 seconds. |
| **Scale** | How big everything is on the screen (100 % is always offered). |
| **Monitors** | Several screens: arrange, mirror, refresh rate (fixed or dynamic), rate on battery, where the bar and dock go, what happens when the lid closes. |
| **Brightness** | The screen's brightness. |
| **Night light** | Warmer colours for the evening: off, warm, warmer, warmest; and *At night* to follow dusk and dawn. |
| **Screenshots** | What the Print key takes, what is in the picture (the bar, the dock, the pointer), copy to the clipboard, show a preview, record with or without sound. |
| **Sound output** | Which speakers or headphones play. |
| **Volume** | The volume, and a volume for each app that is playing. |
| **Equalizer** | The sound's curve for each output, with presets: Flat, Voice, Music, Films, Less bass, More bass. |
| **Microphone** | Which microphone, its level, mute. |

### Connections

| Setting | What you choose |
|---|---|
| **Wi-Fi** | Networks, passwords, hidden networks, a fixed address, sharing the network with a QR code, sharing your connection as a hotspot. |
| **Bluetooth** | Pairing, connecting, sending and receiving files, being visible to others. |
| **VPN** | Add a VPN from the file your provider gives you (WireGuard or OpenVPN), connect, disconnect, forget. |
| **Printers** | Printers and scanners: options, prints waiting, test page, adding one by its address. See [Printers and scanners](#guide-printing). |

**Bluetooth headphones and speakers:** put them in pairing mode, open **Controls → Bluetooth** and click them in the *Nearby* list. If the sound still plays elsewhere once they are connected, click *"Click to send this computer's sound to it"*.

### Power

| Setting | What you choose |
|---|---|
| **Power mode** | Saver, Balanced or Performance. By default: Performance on the charger, Balanced on battery. |
| **Battery** | Its state and health, *Save battery when low*, *Stop charging at 80 %* (on computers that allow it), what uses the most power. |
| **Sleep** | When the screen turns off and when the computer sleeps if left alone; whether closing the lid does nothing. |

### You and your security

| Setting | What you choose |
|---|---|
| **Lock screen** | When the screen locks when left alone; **Lock now**. |
| **Your account** | Your name, your picture, your password, your fingerprints (on computers with a reader). |
| **Privacy** | What is using the microphone or the camera right now; mute the microphone. |
| **Updates** | Whether you are up to date, **Update now**, **Go back** to the version before, the versions kept and what each one changed. See [Updates](#guide-updates). |

### Keys and the system

| Setting | What you choose |
|---|---|
| **Keyboard layout** | One or several layouts (and the keys to switch between them), key repeat speed and delay. |
| **Mouse and touchpad** | Speed, tap to click, how to right-click, natural scrolling, still while typing, left-handed, pointer size. |
| **Natural scrolling** | The page follows your fingers (on by default). |
| **Shortcuts** | A list of the main keyboard shortcuts (the full list is in [Keyboard shortcuts](#guide-shortcuts)). |
| **Accessibility** | Text size, pointer size, magnifier, reduce motion, a screen reader. |
| **Language** | English, Spanish (Latin America or Spain), Portuguese (Brazil or Portugal), French, German, Italian. Apps follow when they next open. |
| **Time zone** | Your time zone (region, then the time it is there, then the place), and a 24 or 12 hour clock. |
| **Notifications** | Do not disturb, quiet hours at night, which apps may pop up. |
| **Default apps** | The app that opens web pages, folders, pictures, documents, text and video. |
| **Startup apps** | The apps that open by themselves when you start. |
| **About StandardOS** | This computer: its model, processor, memory, graphics, how long it has been on, and the system's version. |
| **Documentation** | Opens this documentation. |
| **Storage** | How full the disk is, **Free up space** (empty the Bin, caches, old system versions), your drives: check, format, rename, eject. |
| **Processor** | What uses the processor; force an app to quit. |
| **Memory** | What uses the memory; close an app to free it. |
| **Graphics** | Your graphics cards; which apps run on the faster one; full effects or *No blur* for weak graphics. |
